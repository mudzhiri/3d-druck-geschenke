## Recommendation

**Use POPLOOP as working brand.**

Why:
- Short, international, easy to say
- Product-first (pop culture / drops / loops / fidget motion) — not “3D print”
- Fits drop strategy (“NEW DROP”)
- No obvious collision in light web search for consumer goods brand of this type
- Domain-candidate: `poploop.de` / `poploop.com` — **owner must approve purchase**

## Owner actions before trademark

1. EUIPO eSearch + TMview (classes 16, 20, 28, 35)
2. DPMA search
3. Domain WHOIS for .de / .com / .eu
4. Social handle check (@poploop)
5. Attorney clearance before spend

## Brand voice

- Short. Punchy. Specific.
- Never: “Elevate your experience”, “Where innovation meets…”, “Unleash your imagination”
- Yes: “WE MAKE COOL STUFF.” / “MAKE IT YOURS.” / “SEE IT IRL.”
